Information you send us

If you use the enquiry form, it asks for your name, email address, optional company, service interest and message. The website sends this information to the configured enquiry mailbox so the team can respond. Do not include passwords, payment information or unnecessary sensitive details.

How information is used

Enquiry information is used to review and respond to your request and, where relevant, discuss proposed work. The site should only collect information needed for those purposes.

Storage and retention

Enquiry messages are delivered by email. The business owner must set and document a suitable retention period, access controls and deletion process before launch. This starter site does not include an analytics service or a visitor database.

Cookies and similar technologies

This site does not intentionally set analytics or advertising cookies. Your browser and hosting provider may create technical logs needed to deliver and protect the site. See the cookie information.

Your rights and questions

Privacy rights depend on the circumstances and applicable law. Before publishing, add the verified details of the organisation responsible for this information and a contact route for privacy requests.
