Arixova services / Workflow Automation

Let routine work move between your systems.

We map how work moves through your business, then connect suitable tools so routine updates, notifications and handovers happen more consistently.

The challenge

Does this sound familiar?

  • Information from a form has to be typed into another system.
  • A handover depends on someone remembering to send a message.
  • Approvals get stuck in inboxes without a clear status.
  • Reports take too long to assemble from separate tools.

What we deliver

Practical work.
Clearly scoped.

  • Form-to-system and customer relationship management connections
  • Lead routing, task creation and notifications

  • Testing, documentation and an agreed monitoring approach

A few useful answers

Before we begin.

A walkthrough of the current process, the tools involved and any approval, privacy or audit requirements.

Which apps can you connect?+

It depends on the tools and the integration options they provide. We confirm feasibility before agreeing the build.

What happens when an automation fails?+

We design suitable error notifications and recovery steps so failures can be identified and handled.

Will automation remove the need for staff review?+

Not always. We keep human checks where judgement, approval or sensitive information requires them.

Can you change an existing automated workflow?+

Yes, after reviewing how it works, who depends on it and how failures are currently handled.
