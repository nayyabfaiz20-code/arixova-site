A few useful answers

Before we begin.

Website access, hosting details and a list of the issues or recurring tasks that take up your time.

What does a support plan include?+

The plan is agreed around your platform, priorities and expected requests. The scope and any limits are documented before it starts.

Can you manage a website you did not build?+

Often, yes. We first review how it is built, who hosts it and whether we can safely take responsibility for it.

Do you guarantee that a website will never go down?+

No service can responsibly promise that. We can agree monitoring, backup and response procedures appropriate to your site.

Can I request one-off changes?+

Yes. One-off work can be discussed separately if ongoing support is not the right fit.
