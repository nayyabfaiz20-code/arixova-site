A few useful answers

Before we begin.

A sense of your audience, goals and existing brand or website. We can help shape the brief if those are still taking form.

How much does a website cost?+

The cost depends on the pages, integrations and features involved. We agree a clear scope and quote before work begins.

How long will it take?+

Timing depends on the scope and how quickly content and feedback are available. We set a realistic schedule after discovery.

Can we edit the site ourselves?+

Yes. Where appropriate, we set up a content management system and show your team how to use it.

Who owns the finished website?+

Ownership, access and any third-party licence costs are agreed clearly as part of the project.
